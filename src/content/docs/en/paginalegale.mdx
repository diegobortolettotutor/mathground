<h3 id="privacy">Privacy Policy</h3>

##### Personal data
This site does not collect personal data. Messages sent via email or Telegram remain private between the sender and the site owner and are not used for any other purpose.

##### Analytics
The site uses Umami Cloud for traffic analysis. Umami is cookieless and anonymized by default, so it does not track personally identifiable information.

##### Interactive solvers
Data entered in the interactive solvers (matrices, vectors, etc.) remains completely anonymous.

##### Cookies
This site does not use cookies.
