This tool applies the **Gram-Schmidt procedure** to a set of vectors in
$\mathbb{R}^n$ and returns an **orthogonal basis** (not normalized) of the
space they span.

It is the version to use when only **orthogonality** between the vectors
matters, not their length: at every step the tool keeps the representative
with **reduced integer coordinates**, avoiding square roots and decimal
numbers at every stage of the computation.

Enter the vectors as columns (integers or fractions, e.g. `1/2`, `-3`), choose
how many there are ($k$) and which space they live in ($n$), then press
**SYNTHETIC RESULT** for just the final basis, or **STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION** to
see every intermediate calculation: projections, orthogonal parts, and the
common factor highlighted whenever a simpler-coordinate representative is
chosen.

If one of the vectors turns out to be **linearly dependent** on the previous
ones, the tool flags it and discards it automatically: the final orthogonal
basis will then have fewer than $k$ elements.

**Do not use it for**: sets with more than 8 vectors or in dimension higher
than 8 (the tool's limit), nor to get directly an *orthonormal* basis (with
unit-length versors) — for that there is the
[full Gram-Schmidt solver](/en/risolutori/gram-schmidt/).
