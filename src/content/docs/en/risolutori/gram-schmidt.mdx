This tool applies the **Gram-Schmidt procedure** to a set of vectors in
$\mathbb{R}^n$ and returns an **orthonormal basis** of the space they span.

Enter the vectors as columns (integers or fractions, e.g. `1/2`, `-3`), choose
how many there are ($k$) and which space they live in ($n$), then press
**SYNTHETIC RESULT** for just the final basis, or **STEP-BY-STEP SOLUTION** to
see every intermediate calculation: projections, orthogonal parts, squared
norms and versors, with the common factor highlighted whenever a
simpler-coordinate representative is chosen.

If one of the vectors turns out to be **linearly dependent** on the previous
ones, the tool flags it and discards it automatically: the final orthonormal
basis will then have fewer than $k$ elements.

**Do not use it for**: sets with more than 8 vectors or in dimension higher
than 8 (the tool's limit), nor to get a purely *orthogonal* basis (without
normalization) — for that there is the
[dedicated solver](/en/risolutori/gram-schmidt-solo-orto/), which is faster
because it avoids radicals.
